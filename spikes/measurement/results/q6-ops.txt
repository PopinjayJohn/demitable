=== the corpus pins down `contains` for two different field shapes ===
  tags          is a YAML list of strings: contains is LIST MEMBERSHIP.
                The values are hierarchical (monster/cr/1-4), so under a
                substring reading `contains "monster/cr"` would be a hit
                and `contains "monster/cr/1-4"` a different one.
  obsidianUIMode is a scalar string: contains is SUBSTRING.
  One operator, two semantics, decided by the field's type — which is
  what ADR-0040's type registry is for.
