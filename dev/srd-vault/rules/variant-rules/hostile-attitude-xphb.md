# Hostile [Attitude]
*Source: SRD 5.2 and the Free Rules (2024)* 

A Hostile creature views you unfavorably. You have [Disadvantage](rules/variant-rules/disadvantage-xphb.md) on an ability check to influence a Hostile creature.
