# Boon of the Night Spirit
*Source: SRD 5.2 and the Free Rules (2024)*  

**Prerequisite**: 19th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.

**Merge with Shadows.** While within [Dim Light](rules/variant-rules/dim-light-xphb.md) or [Darkness](rules/variant-rules/darkness-xphb.md), you can give yourself the [Invisible](rules/conditions.md#Invisible) condition as a [Bonus Action](rules/variant-rules/bonus-action-xphb.md). The condition ends on you immediately after you take an action, a [Bonus Action](rules/variant-rules/bonus-action-xphb.md), or a [Reaction](rules/variant-rules/reaction-xphb.md).

**Shadowy Form.** While within [Dim Light](rules/variant-rules/dim-light-xphb.md) or [Darkness](rules/variant-rules/darkness-xphb.md), you have [Resistance](rules/variant-rules/resistance-xphb.md) to all damage except Psychic and Radiant.
