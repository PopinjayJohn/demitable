# Saltmere

The desk for *[[The Salt Debt]]*. Everything the table knows is linked from
here. Everything the table does not know is marked `visibility: gm`, and
it never leaves the GM's machine.

## Where the party has been

- [[The Drowned Quarter]] — the old quarter, half-drowned, where the
  salt-panners keep the tall pans and the fish-market keeps its smell.

## Who the party is counting on

- [[Hessa Cray]] — factor for the Brinebacks, and the only person in
  Saltmere who has not yet decided what to do about them.
- [[Bel Orrery]] — the pilot of the *Gannet*. He came up. He did not come
  back.

## What is owed

- [[Pay the Tide]] — four hundred marks, due at the spring tide.
