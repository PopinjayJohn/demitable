# Saltmere Faces

Six faces for the people who might be in the doorway of the Tide House when
you knock. Roll for who it is. The table is deliberately weighted towards
faces who want something.

**At the table.** Roll when a player walks into a room the crew occupies.
The face is whoever is there, and it brings its own business: a runner
with a message, a captain with an offer, a tide-reader with a warning. If
the roll lands on *whoever is in the doorway*, that person is not in the
Tide House at all, the room is empty, and somebody is watching from the
lane.

## The regulars

- **Hessa Cray** — the factor. Brings terms.
- **Ballow Fenn** — captain of the boat that replaced the *Gannet*, and
  the only one of the eleven who has ever been to sea in a Crown cutter.
- **Nock** — muscle, and not stupid, which is the dangerous combination.
- **Meighan Sarl** — the runner who found the party. She owes Hessa Cray
  eleven marks herself.
- **Old Fenner** — reads the tide by eye and gets it right more often
  than the jar does.

> [!secret] Who is not in the table
> Corvin Sallow is not a face. If he is ever in the room, the table is
> wrong, and the roll is the GM's decision and not the dice's. ^sallowface
