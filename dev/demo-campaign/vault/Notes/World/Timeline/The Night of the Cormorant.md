# The Night of the Cormorant

The night of 13 March. Fog off the seaward marsh from the second hour of
the evening, thick enough to turn the Causeway lamps into a row of
yellow ghosts. The tide was making at half past nine, which is the hour
the *Gannet* was under the Causeway arch, and the tide was not why she
went down.

What the city knows: a lighter onto the Causeway shoulder in the fog, salt
in the water, and by morning a white line along the tide wall a mile long.
The reeve's men were out early.

What the party know: they were in it.
