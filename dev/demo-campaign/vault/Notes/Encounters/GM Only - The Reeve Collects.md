# GM Only — The Reeve Collects

*An encounter for whenever the party try to leave Saltmere, settle the
debt, or open the crate they were told not to open.*

## The situation

The reeve's two men come to the Tide House on the morning after the salt
was found. They are polite and they are wet and they want to look at the
crate, and if the party let them look at the crate they will take it, and
with it the only evidence that the salt was ever theirs.

The reeve himself does not come. The reeve never comes.

## The three ways it goes

1. **They let him in.** The crate goes to the Crown wharf, the salt is
   counted, and four hundred marks become five hundred, because the
   reeve's price for quiet is never the same twice.
2. **They keep him out.** He leaves, and by noon every dock in Saltmere
   knows the party are holding salt for somebody, and the Brinebacks have
   to decide what that means for the Roll.
3. **They make him an offer.** The best version and the most dangerous:
   the party pay the reeve instead of Hessa Cray, the debt changes hands,
   and Hessa Cray finds out within the week.

## What the reeve wants

Not the salt. The salt is Sallow's problem. The reeve wants to be able to
write the word *sold* on the salt, which requires a buyer at the wharf,
and Sallow needs the reeve to be able to write that word.

## Stakes

If the party are clever here, they learn that the salt has a buyer before
it has a price, and that is the thread that leads to a man in a grey coat
who is never in Saltmere.

## Do not run this twice

If the party have already dealt with the reeve, the second half of the
campaign changes shape. See [[Corvin Sallow]].
