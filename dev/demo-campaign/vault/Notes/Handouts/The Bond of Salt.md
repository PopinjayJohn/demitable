# The Bond of Salt

*One page, not written in ink, and not signed yet. Hessa Cray puts it on
the table and does not push it across.*

---

Saltmere, the thirteenth day of the month of the tide.

We, the undersigned, having taken the salt of this house over the water of
the Causeway, do owe to the house of Cray four hundred salt-marks, at the
spring tide, and to be paid in such coin as the house shall name.

Should the marks not be paid, then the name of the debtor shall be
written upon the Roll at the head of the Causeway, and shall be read at
every tide, and shall remain until the marks are paid.

The tide does not wait. Neither do we.

— *the house of Cray*

---

*Only two names are written at the foot of it: Ilva Sarn and Orson Pike.
The third member of the campaign was not in the room when the ink went
round, and is not on the Roll, and that is the difference between a debt
and a company of debtors.*
