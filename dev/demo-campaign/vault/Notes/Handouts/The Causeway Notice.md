# The Causeway Notice

*Nailed to the tide wall at the head of the Causeway, and to nine other
walls in the city, in a hand that is not the reeve's.*

---

**BY ORDER OF THE REEVE OF THE SALT TITHE**

The Causeway is closed to the passage of salt at the third hour before
high water, and to the passage of anything else at all, until the
spring tide shall have passed.

Salt found in the water is salt that has been sold. Salt found in the
water is the property of the Crown, and any person who shall have
received salt in the water, or shall have been the cause of salt in the
water, is guilty of an offence and shall be reported to this office at
the Salt Gate.

Persons are paid for what they carry, and for what they say they carry,
and for what they are seen carrying. Persons who are paid nothing are
paid twice.

— *by order of the reeve*

---

The notice has been up since the day after the wreck. It is the reason
the party have not yet been able to leave Saltmere by road, and the
reason the Brinebacks' boats look so busy.
