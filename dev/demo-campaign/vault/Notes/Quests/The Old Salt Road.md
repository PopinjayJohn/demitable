# The Old Salt Road

Last winter's problem, and closed.

The old salt road is the cart track along the north dyke, which is a road
for three days of the month and standing water for the other twenty-seven.
The Crown's reeve closed it on a technicality — a charter of 1804 that
grants the right of passage to boats, read as boats — and the
salt-panners had no way to move their pans.

The party took the reeve's own clerks' measurements and made him reopen
it. It is a footnote now. The reeve has not forgotten who did it, and
neither have the party.
