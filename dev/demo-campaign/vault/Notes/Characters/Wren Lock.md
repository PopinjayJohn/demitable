# Wren Lock

*A draft. The player is still writing this one and the GM has not read it
yet — so nobody else has either.*

Wren Lock rows the ferry across the Salt Gate channel for a living, which
in Saltmere is a respectable trade with an unsavoury reputation, because
the ferry only runs at the hours when the marsh road is under water and
everybody on it is somewhere they would rather not be.

The character is not settled. The occupation is ferryman, and the player is
considering something else, and there is an argument in progress about
whether Wren was in Saltmere before the Causeway was built at all — a
question the player has not yet needed to answer and the GM has very much
enjoyed not being asked.
