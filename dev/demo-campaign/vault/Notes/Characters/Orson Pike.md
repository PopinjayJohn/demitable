# Orson Pike

A Crown surveyor, which in Saltmere means a person employed to discover
things that are already known and to write them down in a form that can
be used against somebody. He has been four years in the marsh office and
he has the good handwriting and the bad coat.

He came to Saltmere for the Causeway resurvey and he has been in the
campaign office since January, going over charts the Crown made in 1911
and finding, occasionally, that they are not wrong about the marsh but are
wrong about who has been in it.

He keeps a route of his own that he has not told the party about, and he
has not decided why he has not told them. If you ask him, he will give you
a survey answer, and it will be a good one.

He is the only one of the party who has been to the fish-market and
bought a drink.

> line that does not match anything else he has, and he has not decided what
