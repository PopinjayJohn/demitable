# Corvin Sallow

The man in the grey coat. He has never been in Saltmere and he has been
in Saltmere for eleven years.

Sallow buys untaxed salt. Not from the Brinebacks — from whoever has it,
at a better price than the Brinebacks will take, which is only possible if
you know how much the Brinebacks have and when they will move it. He owns
a house on the coast road, a share of the causeway toll, and the man who
counts the salt at the Crown wharf.

He is not a smuggler and would be offended by the word. He is a
*purchaser*, and the law he breaks is a law about who may sell, and he is
careful to break it in the direction of the market.

He bought the reeve in March. He paid the reeve to look at the wrong
chart, and the fog did the rest. He is the reason the party are standing
in a salt marsh at three in the morning, and he has never met them and
has no intention of doing so.

**If the party meet him**, they will not know him. He will be the
questionable factor with the wet coat, and he will offer to settle the
debt, and the offer will be genuine and ruinous.
