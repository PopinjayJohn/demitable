# Hessa Cray

The Brinebacks' factor. Eleven years in the Tide House, which is long
enough to have outlived two Crown reeves and short enough that she is
still the only person in Saltmere who does what she does.

She is not the owner. The Brinebacks are a crew and the crew does not have
one, and Hessa Cray is the woman who keeps the ledger and decides what a
name on the Roll is worth. She has never once been seen to want anything
for herself, and the whole city has decided that this is because she does
not need anything, which is the most dangerous conclusion anyone in
Saltmere has ever reached about anybody.

She drinks tea like water and talks about the weather the way other people
confess. In the last year she has been slower to smile and quicker to stop
talking, and the crew have noticed.
