# Bel Orrery

The pilot of the *Gannet*. Forty-one years old, twenty-two of them on the
salt runs, and the only man in Saltmere who could take a lighter through
the Causeway arch in fog.

He came up. That is the whole of what the party know about him at first:
a coat and a face in the reeds, and then the two hours of the night spent
on the Causeway wall shouting at the water while the fog came back.

He was still holding the jar when they found him. Nobody has asked him
what he was doing with it, because he was not able to answer, and because
asking would have meant saying the question out loud in front of the
party.

He is buried at the low end of the Drowned Quarter, on the fish-market
side, in a hole that fills at every tide above the third hour. The party
should go at low water, and they should go alone, and nobody should
mention the jar.
