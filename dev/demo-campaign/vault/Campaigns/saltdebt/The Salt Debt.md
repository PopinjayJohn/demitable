# The Salt Debt

*A salt-marsh port, a smuggling crew, and a debt that comes due.*

Saltmere stands where the sea comes in twice a day and goes out twice a
day. The only dry road out of it is the Crown's Causeway, and the
Causeway is taxed. Salt is the one thing the marsh makes, and untaxed
salt is worth three times taxed salt, and the difference between those
two numbers is the whole economy — and it belongs to a crew called **the
Brinebacks**.

The party took a job in the fog, lost a lighter's worth of untaxed salt
into the water, and woke up owing four hundred marks to the Brinebacks'
factor. They do not know yet that the salt was meant to be lost. They
know that the ledger comes due at the spring tide, and that a name on the
Brine ledger in Saltmere is a kind of drowning that takes a year.
