# Session 01 — The Cormorant Wreck

*14 March. Four hours, one fog, one lighter lost.*

## What happened

Hessa Cray's runner found the party at the Salt Gate with a sealed crate
and a fee of eleven marks, and the instruction was short: carry it to the
Tide House on Sallow Lane, do not stop, do not ask what is in it.

The fog came down on the Causeway about an hour in, and the party took
the channel that goes south toward the Drowned Quarter when the channel
that goes north was the right one. The lighter — a shallow-draft
*Gannet* — found the shoulder of the Causeway at speed.

The crate went into the water first and broke. The salt came out of it in
a white bloom and spread across the flat, white on black water, visible
from the Causeway wall for a mile. That is the part that cannot be taken
back: not the wreck, the *colour*.

Two things came out of the water that were not in the crate. The pilot,
[[Bel Orrery]], came up. And a jar.

The jar is brass, has a glass belly, and does not open. It is now in the
party's hands, and Hessa Cray has sent word that the party owes her four
hundred marks, which is what it cost to keep the reeve's mouth shut about
a white bloom on black water.

## Where the party are

At the Tide House, in the dry, with Hessa Cray making tea and talking
about the weather. The bond is not signed. The jar is on the table
between them.

## Loose ends

- The jar. Nobody can open it, and Hessa Cray has not explained what it
  is.
- The white bloom. It was on the water for an hour and a half, and there
  is a road above the Causeway wall.
- [[Orson Pike]]'s survey. He has been staring at the Causeway charts
  since they came out of the fog, and he has not said why.
- [[Ilva Sarn]] has not mentioned that she took the job.

## Next time

The reeve's men come to the Tide House in the morning. Hessa Cray will
offer terms before they arrive, and she will offer them as a kindness.
